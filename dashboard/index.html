Provider Tax Cap Exposure

Expansion states with non-exempt provider taxes above the new 3.5% ceiling — at-risk revenue as the cap phases down FFY2028–2032. Nursing facility and ICF/IID taxes are exempt from the phase-down; only hospital, MCO, ambulance, and "other" classes are subject.

Work Requirements — Modeled Coverage Loss

CBO estimates ~10 million coverage losses nationally. Most subject adults already work or qualify for exemptions; modeled loss is driven by administrative churn, not refusal to work (calibrated to Arkansas 2018).
